---
title: Getting started
description: Create your account, your workspace and your first project.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create your account

Sign up with your email address or a social login. Confirm your email address from the message you receive.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create a workspace

A workspace holds your projects and your team. Give it your company or team name. On the Free plan you can own one workspace; you can still join workspaces owned by others.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create a project

Open **Projects** and choose **New project**. Give it a name, and optionally a start and end date. Every project opens on its overview, with tabs for the list, board, calendar and timeline views.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add tasks

Add tasks from the list or the board. Each task can have an assignee, a priority, labels, a start date, a due date and a progress percentage. Mark a task as a milestone when it represents a key date rather than work.

</Step>
<Step>

### Invite your team

Open the workspace settings and invite members by email. They receive an invitation link and join with the role you choose.

</Step>
</Steps>
