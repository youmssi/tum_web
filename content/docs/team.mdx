## Workspaces

A workspace contains projects, members and a billing plan. You can belong to several workspaces and switch between them from the workspace menu. Each workspace has its own plan.

## Roles

| Role   | Can                                                          |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Owner  | Do everything, including billing and deleting the workspace. |
| Admin  | Manage members, projects and settings.                       |
| Member | Work on the projects they can access.                        |

Owners and admins change a member's role from the members list in the workspace settings.

## Inviting members

Invite people by email from the workspace settings. The invitation link is valid for a limited time; you can send a new one if it expires. On the Free plan a workspace can have up to five members.

## Restricted projects

A project can be limited to its own members. Other workspace members do not see it, except admins and the owner.

## Audit log

On the Enterprise plan, the audit log records every change made in the workspace. You can review it and export it.
