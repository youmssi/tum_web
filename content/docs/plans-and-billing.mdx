Plans apply to a workspace, not to a person. Every member of a paid workspace gets its features.

Prices on the website are shown in US dollars. At checkout you pay the equivalent amount in your local currency, CFA franc (XAF) by default.

## Paying

Payments are processed by [Dexpay](https://dexpay.africa). You can pay by card (Visa, Mastercard) or with the other methods Dexpay offers in your country.

1. Open **Billing** in the workspace settings. Only the owner can do this.
2. Choose a plan and a billing period.
3. Complete the payment on the Dexpay page. You return to Tûm when it is done, and the plan is active right away.

You receive an email confirming the activation.

## Renewal

Subscriptions do not renew silently. Before each renewal Dexpay sends the billing email a message with a payment link. If you do not pay by the due date, the plan stays active for seven more days. After that, the workspace returns to the Free plan.

## Returning to the Free plan

Your data is kept. Projects and members above the Free limits stay in place, but you cannot create new projects or invite new members until you are back under the limits or upgrade again. Pro and Enterprise views become read-only prompts to upgrade.

## Self-hosted installations

Self-hosted installations have every feature with no limits. See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting).
